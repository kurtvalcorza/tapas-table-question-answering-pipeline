## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) answered the synthetic city table and reported `sample-sanity` denotation accuracy on three authored questions. This notebook keeps that table and its inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real labelled table-question corpus fetched with a digest pin and executed for its gold answers, a classification protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (denotation, aggregation and cell accuracy, per category and per operator), two non-neural baselines, a bounded fine-tuning with the checkpoint's own weak-supervision loss, a per-type breakdown, the city table re-answered after adaptation, and an artifact that reloads. The per-grid `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 6-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the tokenizer or the model is constructed; `TAPASTableQAPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `pyarrow`, `safetensors`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or the model's tokenizer directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample (`samples.py`) is TAT-QA lookups plus FinQA sums over company financial-report tables, from datasets not named in the checkpoint's documented SQA → WikiSQL → WTQ fine-tuning chain (content overlap was not audited): `tatqa_dataset_train.json` from the Hub dataset `next-tat/TAT-QA` at revision `c96247f5077eac447f63527fd3dcfdc58bb56d6a` (CC BY 4.0) and FinQA's `dataset/{train,dev,test}.json` from `github.com/czyssrs/FinQA` at commit `0f16e2867befa6840783e58be38c9efb9229d742` (MIT; its Hub card says CC BY 4.0, so it is attributed as CC BY 4.0 too), each fetched whole and refused on any byte-count or SHA-256 mismatch (`CORPORA`). Every gold lies in one column, because the pinned WTQ config has `select_one_column: true` (BYOD golds are held to the same rule); a TAT-QA span must occur once in the table, and every term of a FinQA addition chain must match one cell inside FinQA's evidence rows and agree with FinQA's displayed answer within its rounding. The seeded draw is FinQA-report-disjoint and TAT-QA-table-disjoint (cross-source report overlap not checked): 130 / 36 / 60 questions (train lookup 80, sum 50; validation 18 and test 30 per type), and every sampled question is tokenized to confirm nothing is truncated at 512 tokens. **COUNT and AVERAGE are not exercised by the default sample** (no single-column source for either). Nothing is redistributed by this repository. DAT9: neither source is named in the documented fine-tuning chain; content overlap was not audited, and possible exposure of the BERT backbone's pre-training to public financial text was not checked.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` (per pinned file) + `sample_candidates` (`tatqa_candidates`, `finqa_candidates`) + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` + `answer` with sanity checks + `evaluation_report` on the city table (inference contract) → `first_cell_baseline` + `keyword_lookup_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with the per-type breakdown (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → `answer` + `evaluation_report` on the city table again (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `TAPASTableQAPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** the cell decision is a fixed sigmoid threshold, the operator an argmax, neither a calibrated probability, and the model never abstains; the numeric answer is the pipeline's arithmetic; denotation, aggregation and cell accuracy are own implementations and none is a human judgement; no acceptance threshold ships. The city table re-answered after adaptation carries a `sample-sanity` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the first-cell floor, the keyword lookup and the frozen model are scored on the same 60 test questions as the adapted model, on value-equality and strict-operator accuracy (co-primary) plus operator and cell accuracy, per question type; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample with no dispersion estimate. The notebook makes no claim about the outcome before a hosted run, separates workflow execution, reload parity and held-out performance, teaches a no-gain result as valid, and discloses that question type and source are confounded (lookups are TAT-QA, sums are FinQA).
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and table-disjoint; validation (90 questions) selects the epoch on the mean of the three measures and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that tables from few documents must be split by source.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MAX_ROWS` (64), `MAX_COLUMNS` (32), `MAX_TOKENS` (512, truncates), `MAX_QUERY_CHARS` (500), `MAX_CELL_CHARS` (200), `AGGREGATIONS`, `CELL_THRESHOLD`, the record bounds (8..20,000) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one input refusal are demonstrated, and a record whose gold cells lie past the truncation point is refused at training time.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one JSONL file (or JSON list) is read into the runtime and its records flow through the same validation, table-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected schema and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split is written to `outputs/tapas_table_qa_train.jsonl` in the same shape.
- Licence: the weights are Apache-2.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; TAT-QA (CC BY 4.0) and FinQA (MIT; CC BY 4.0 on its Hub card) are not redistributed — the export records the repository, revision, file, bytes, digest, licence and the sample digest under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.
